Misc guitar tools

  • Fretboard Scale Finder — click notes on the neck to find matching scales, or pick a root and scale to light it up, with chords and playback.
  • Exotic Scale Finder — the same tool with Spanish, Japanese, Arabic, and other scales added.
  • Piano Scale Finder — the same tool on a two-octave piano keyboard, with the root note in red and the rest of the keyboard greyed out around the scale.
  • Circle of Fifths Guitar Explorer — drag the circle of fifths and the guitar neck lights up the scale of the selected key, with its diatonic chords.
  • CAGED Position Boxes — the five CAGED scale patterns tiling the neck, for major scale, major pentatonic, and minor pentatonic.
  • Guitar Treble Clef Note Trainer: treble clef staff above the guitar neck, with a show mode and a staff-to-neck quiz, sounds, and a range from middle C to C6.
  • Song Key Finder: type in a song's chords and it ranks the keys they fit, shows the scales that work, and lights the scale on a guitar neck.
