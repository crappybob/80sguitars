80s Guitar Episodes

Chronicles of 1980s guitars, told in installments. Each guitar's story starts with the purchase and continues in later updates as the work does: the research, the surprises, the parts hunts, the repairs. The hard facts (measurements, serial decodes, factory part numbers) are woven into the story and collected at the end of each installment for anyone in a hurry. Most of it was published because it could not be found anywhere else.

The guitars

  • Yamaha RGX112 (Taiwan, 1987). A beat-up guitar in unknown working condition, a $250 asking price, and a $75 open offer. Part 1 is up; the rest of its story is still happening.
