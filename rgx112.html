Serial number decoder (Taiwan)

Status: Unverified. This is the published scheme for Taiwan-built Yamahas of this era. The worked example below is consistent with this model's catalog production window, but the scheme itself has not been confirmed against factory records.

Format: two letters, then five digits.

PositionMeaning
First letterLast digit of the year: H=1, I=2, J=3, K=4, L=5, M=6, N=7, O=8, P=9, Q=0
Second letterMonth: H=January, I=February, J=March, K=April, L=May, M=June, N=July, O=August, P=September, X=October, Y=November, Z=December
Digits 1-2Day of the month
Remaining digitsUnit number

Worked example: NN18083 reads as N=7 (1987), N=July, day 18, unit 83.
