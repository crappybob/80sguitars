CAGED Position Boxes

The CAGED system names five scale patterns after the open chord shapes C, A, G, E, and D. The boxes tile the neck: the top of one box is the bottom of the next, so you can move up the neck without ever leaving the key. Pick a key and a scale, then click a box below the neck to isolate it. In an isolated box, the chord tones of that shape are ringed.

Standard tuning, high E on top. Root notes are red. Boxes are four-fret windows; neighboring boxes share border frets, which is how they connect. This page works offline as a single file.
